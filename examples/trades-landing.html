Questions

The stuff owners ask first

Does it really handle prevailing wage and certified payroll?
Yes — that's the whole point. Crewtally computes the make‑up wage between your shop rate and the wage determination, blends overtime at the weighted‑average regular rate, annualizes fringe, and produces a filing‑ready WH‑347 for each public job. It's built for Davis‑Bacon and California prevailing wage, not bolted on.
What if someone clocks in from the wrong place?
The punch still counts — we never lose someone's time. It's recorded with how far off site it was and flagged for the foreman to review. A job with no geofence, or a phone that won't share location, is simply accepted and marked unverified.
Can one person be paid at two different rates in a week?
Yes. Log the hours under each role — say plumber and foreman — and each is paid at its own rate. When overtime hits, it's calculated on the blended, weighted‑average regular rate the way the law requires.
How does billing work with seasonal help?
You're billed $5 for each active employee per month. Give a seasonal helper an end date and they roll off the roster automatically — and stop counting toward your bill — the day the season ends.
Where does my data live?
This build runs on your own machine with penny‑accurate integer math — no cents lost to floating point, no crew list sitting on someone else's server. Texts send for real once you connect an SMS carrier; until then the nudge preview shows you exactly what would go out.
